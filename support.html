Common questions

How do I connect to my coach?
Your coach texts you an invite code. Tap I have an invite code on the first screen and enter it during sign up.

An Instagram reel says "Watch this reel in the app".
Tap Continue on web at the top right and the reel plays right there. If Instagram asks you to log in or sign up, tap the X. You don't need an Instagram account.

I forgot my password.
On the sign in screen tap Forgot password?. We email you a link. Tap it, pick a new password, then sign in.

How do I add another player?
Coaches tap the plus on the dashboard to add a player. Families with a second child ask their coach for another invite code.

How do I report or block someone?
Press and hold their message in Chat and choose Report or Block. To report a video or note, open it and tap the flag at the top. You can see and undo blocks in Profile, Blocked people.

How do I delete my account?
Open Profile and tap Delete account at the bottom. This permanently deletes your account, your information and your files. It can't be undone.

Is Sidebar free?
Sidebar is free for parents and players.
