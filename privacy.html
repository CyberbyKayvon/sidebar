Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

The short version. Sidebar is a private space for a tennis coach, their junior players and the players' parents. We only collect what's needed to run it. We never sell your information, never show ads, and never track you across other apps or websites. Parents are in charge of their juniors' information.

Sidebar is run by Coach Kayvon ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Sidebar handles, why, and the choices you have. Questions? Email cyberbykayvon@gmail.com.

Who uses Sidebar

  • Coaches create an account and share clips, links, notes and lesson plans.
  • Parents and players join with an invite code from their coach and see what that coach shares for the player.
  • Players under 13 can only join through the invite code the coach sent to their parent or guardian. By using the code, the parent or guardian confirms they agree to this policy for their child. Players 13 and older can have their own login.

Information we handle

  • Account details: your name and email address. For coaches, the name of your coaching business.
  • Player profiles: the first name, age and level a coach enters for each player. We don't ask for a player's last name, photo, phone number or location.
  • Things you share: videos you upload, links you paste, lesson notes, lesson plans, progress notes and chat messages.
  • Safety reports you send, and the list of people you've blocked.

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising identifiers or browsing history. Sidebar only sees videos you pick yourself. It never has access to the rest of your photo library.

How we use it

  • To run Sidebar: showing a family what their coach shared, delivering chat messages, and keeping your account working.
  • To keep people safe: reviewing reports and enforcing our community rules.
  • To answer your questions when you contact us.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

Who can see what

  • Everything a coach shares with a family is visible to that family's parents and juniors, and to the coach.
  • Family chat is one shared thread. Parents see every message. There are no private messages between a coach and a junior.
  • Families can't see other families' content.
  • Safety reports go to us, not to the person you reported.

Where your information is stored

Your information is stored with Supabase, a database and file hosting service, on servers in the United States. It is encrypted when it travels between your phone and the server and when it sits in storage. Access rules in the database make sure each family only reaches its own coach's content for its own player, and nobody else can open it. A copy is also kept in Sidebar's private storage on your phone, which is excluded from iCloud backup. If you forget your password, we email you a reset link through the same service.

Other services

  • YouTube: when you add a YouTube link, Sidebar asks YouTube for the video's title. When you play a YouTube video, it plays in YouTube's embedded player, and YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that video.
  • Instagram: Instagram reels open on Instagram's website inside Sidebar. Instagram's privacy policy applies there. You don't need an Instagram account.
  • Apple: Apple delivers the app and may share crash reports with us only if you've turned that on in your iPhone's settings.

Children's privacy

Sidebar is meant for coaches and families. We do not knowingly let a child under 13 create an account without a parent or guardian. Parents can review everything shared with their player at any time, and can delete the account, which deletes the player's information too. We never show ads to anyone, never track children, and never share a child's information with third parties. If you believe a child's information was added without a parent's permission, contact us at cyberbykayvon@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting information

  • You can delete your account any time in Sidebar: Profile, Delete account. That immediately and permanently removes your login, your information and your files from our servers and from your phone.
  • Coaches can remove a single player in the app, which deletes that player's content.
  • Coaches can remove any clip, link or note they shared.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, passwords are stored hashed and never readable by us, and database access rules limit every account to its own family or coach. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a passcode on your phone and a password you do not use anywhere else.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, export or delete your information. You can do most of this in the app. For anything else, email cyberbykayvon@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
