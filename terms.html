Terms and Community Rules

Last updated October 12, 2026

The short version. Sidebar is a safe, private space for coaches, juniors and parents. Be respectful, only share what you have the right to share, and keep it about tennis. We have zero tolerance for abuse or anything that puts a child at risk.

These terms are an agreement between you and Coach Kayvon. By creating an account you agree to them and to our Privacy Policy.

Accounts

  • Coaches and parents must be 18 or older.
  • Players 13 and older may have their own login. Players under 13 join only through the invite code sent to their parent or guardian, who is responsible for their account.
  • Keep your login private. You're responsible for what happens in your account.

Community rules

Everyone using Sidebar agrees not to post or send anything that:

  • is sexual, violent, hateful, bullying, harassing or threatening
  • puts a child's safety or privacy at risk, including sharing a junior's personal details with people outside their family and coach
  • is spam, a scam, or advertising unrelated to coaching
  • you don't have the right to share, such as someone else's video uploaded without their permission
  • breaks the law

Coaches also agree to keep all communication with juniors in the shared family chat, where parents can see it.

Reporting and blocking

You can report any message, video or note, and block anyone in your chat. Press and hold a message, or tap the flag on a video or note. We review reports within 24 hours. When something breaks these rules, we remove it, and we may suspend or remove the account that posted it. If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 first.

Your content

You own what you share. You give us permission to store it and show it to the people you share it with, only so Sidebar works. When you remove something or delete your account, we delete it.

Links to YouTube and Instagram are shown using those services' own players and pages, and their terms apply to that content. Credit creators, and only upload someone else's video if they've given you permission.

Speed Lab

Ball speeds in Speed Lab are estimates. They aren't checked against a radar gun and shouldn't be relied on for anything important.

The service

We're working hard on Sidebar, but it's provided as is, and it may change or have downtime. To the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for indirect losses from using it. You can stop using Sidebar and delete your account at any time. We may suspend accounts that break these rules.
